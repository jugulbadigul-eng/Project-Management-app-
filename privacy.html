Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. What is Project Hub?

Project Hub is an internal project management tool used to track projects, tasks, milestones, issues, and team activities for authorised users within an organisation.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide the service:

  • Account information: Your email address and name when you register.
  • Usage data: Projects, tasks, and activity you create or update within the app.
  • Authentication tokens: Stored securely in your browser to keep you signed in.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to provide the Project Hub service. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Authentication

Authentication is handled by Supabase, a secure backend-as-a-service. Optional Google Sign-In uses Google's official OAuth 2.0 flow and redirects you directly to Google — we never see your Google password.

5. Data Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Access to project data is restricted to authenticated users only. We take reasonable technical precautions to protect your information.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact the site administrator.
